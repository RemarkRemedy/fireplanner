| HDB void deck rental | $0-$100 | $100 | $100 |
| Funeral parlour venue rental | $500/day | $1,000/day | $1,800/day |

| Obituary notice | $1,348 | $1,500 | $2,000+ |
| Certificate of Cause of Death (home death) | $200 | $225 | $250 |

**Cremation vs Burial:**
- Cremation dominant in Singapore (>80% of deaths). Government cremation at Mandai costs only $100.
- Burial land-constrained to 15 years, then exhumation mandatory. Religious burial $315-$940.
- Practical total for cremation path: package + coffin + $100 cremation + $500-$750 niche = roughly $5,500-$8,500.

## 3. Government Subsidies and Grants

- **CPF Funeral Expense Claim:** Up to **$6,000** from deceased's un-nominated CPF monies
- **ComCare Short-to-Medium Term Assistance:** For low-income families (per-capita income < $1,900/month), case-by-case
- **SGSCOOP Funeral Fund Grant:** $300 for member death; $200 for spouse death (civil servants only)
- **No dedicated government funeral grant** for general population. CPF reimbursement ($6,000 max) is the primary offset.

## 4. Recommended Default for Financial Planning

| Scenario | Total All-In Cost |
|---|---|
| Direct cremation only (no wake) | $1,500-$3,000 |
| Simple 3-day void deck, secular/Christian | $5,000-$7,000 |
| Standard 3-day void deck, Buddhist | $7,000-$9,000 |
| Standard 3-day void deck, Taoist | $9,000-$12,000 |
| Premium parlour, Buddhist with rituals | $12,000-$18,000 |
| Premium parlour, Taoist elaborate | $15,000-$25,000+ |

**Recommended default: $10,000** as the base assumption for a spouse death event.

This represents a 3-day standard wake (void deck or modest parlour), a mid-range coffin, government cremation, and a government or lower-cost private columbarium niche. Includes catering (~50 guests at $25/head = $1,250) and miscellaneous costs. Rounded up slightly from the $8,232 average to build in inflation buffer.

**$15,000 slightly overstates** the median but is not unreasonable as a conservative upper bound for Taoist households or parlour venues.

**Original estimate of $10K-$15K: Confirmed as reasonable range. Use $10K as default.**
