# CPF Schemes for Singaporeans Aged 50-70: Comprehensive Research

**Research date:** 2026-03-29
**Sources:** CPF Board, IRAS, HDB, MOM, MOH official sites + financial blogs (Endowus, DBS, GrowBeansprout, SingSaver)

---

## AGE 50: Contribution Rate Shift

### CPF Contribution Rates (from 1 Jan 2026, wages > $750/month)

| Age Band | Employee | Employer | Total | OA | SA/RA | MA |
|----------|----------|----------|-------|-----|-------|------|
| 50-55 | 20% | 17% | 37% | 15% | 11.5% | 10.5% |
| 55-60 | 18% | 16% | 34% | 12% | 11.5% (RA) | 10.5% |
| 60-65 | 12.5% | 12.5% | 25% | 3.5% | 11% (RA) | 10.5% |
| 65-70 | 7.5% | 9% | 16.5% | 1% | 5% (RA) | 10.5% |
| 70+ | 5% | 7.5% | 12.5% | 1% | 1% (RA) | 10.5% |

- 2026 change: Rates for 55-60 increased to 34% (up 1.5pp); 60-65 increased to 25% (up 1pp). All increases go to RA.
- OW ceiling raised from $7,400 to $8,000/month in 2026 ($96,000/year).
- AW ceiling: $102,000 total (OW + AW combined).

### Workfare Income Supplement (WIS)
- Eligibility: Singapore Citizen, gross monthly income $500-$3,000
- Ages 45-59: Max $4,200/year; Ages 60+: Max $4,900/year
- Split: 60% CPF, 40% cash
- Automatic for eligible workers

## AGE 55: The Big Transition

### 1. RA Creation (Mandatory, one-time)
- RA automatically created
- SA permanently closed
- Transfer order: SA -> RA first, then OA -> RA, up to FRS
- Excess SA above FRS flows to OA (withdrawable)
- RA earns 4% p.a. (floor guaranteed through Dec 2026)

### 2. Retirement Sums (2026)

| Sum | Amount | Est. CPF LIFE Monthly (from 65) |
|-----|--------|-------------------------------|
| BRS | $110,200 | ~$950/month |
| FRS | $220,400 | ~$1,780/month |
| ERS | $440,800 (4x BRS) | ~$3,440/month |

- BRS = half of FRS (for property pledgers)
- ERS = 4x BRS (raised from 3x in 2025)
- ~3.5% annual growth

### 3. Withdrawal at 55
- RA >= FRS: Withdraw all excess OA freely
- RA < FRS: Can still withdraw up to $5,000
- Property pledge: Only need BRS in RA (50% reduction)
- Property pledge refund: When sold, refund pledged amount + accrued interest to RA

### 4. Post-55 Top-ups

**Cash top-ups (RSTU):**
- Cap: Current ERS minus RA balance
- Tax relief: $8,000/year self + $8,000 family = $16,000 max
- 2025 change: Cash top-ups triggering MRSS no longer qualify for RSTU (no double-dipping)

**OA-to-RA transfers:**
- Voluntary, up to ERS
- Can set up automatic monthly transfers
- No tax relief
- Irreversible

### 5. Matched Retirement Savings Scheme (MRSS)
- Eligibility: 55+, SC, RA < BRS, avg monthly income <= $4,000, AV <= $21,000, <= 1 property
- Dollar-for-dollar on cash top-ups to RA
- Annual cap: $2,000/year; Lifetime cap: $20,000
- Match credited to RA in Q1 of following year
- ~750,000 eligible in 2026

### 6. Interest Rates (55+)

**Base rates:** OA 2.5%, RA/MA 4%

**Extra interest (all ages):** +1% on first $60K combined (OA capped at $20K)

**Additional extra interest (55+ only):**
- +2% on first $30K combined (OA capped at $20K)
- +1% on next $30K
- Evaluation order: RA -> OA (cap $20K) -> SA -> MA

**Effective rates:**
- RA first $30K: up to 6%
- RA next $30K: up to 5%
- RA above $60K: 4%
- OA first $20K (within $30K tier): up to 5.5%

Extra interest on OA goes to RA (for 55+).

### 7. BHS and MediSave Overflow
- BHS 2026: $79,000
- At 65: BHS freezes for life
- Overflow: MA above BHS -> RA (up to FRS) -> OA
- MediShield Life + CareShield Life premiums deducted from MA

### 8. Voluntary Housing Refund (VHR)
- Refund OA money previously used for housing (principal + accrued interest)
- Post-55: Refunds go to RA first (up to FRS), excess to OA
- Higher CPF interest on refunded amounts
- Reduces future refund obligation at property sale
- Partial or full refunds accepted

### 9. CPF Transfers to Spouse
- After setting aside BRS, can transfer excess OA or RA to spouse's RA
- Spouse's RA can receive up to ERS
- Irreversible, no tax relief

## AGE 62-64: SRS Withdrawal Window

### Supplementary Retirement Scheme (SRS)
- Contribution cap: $15,300/year (SC/PR), $35,700 (foreigners)
- Penalty-free withdrawal age: Statutory retirement age at time of FIRST contribution
  - SRA was 62 until Jun 2022, 63 from Jul 2022, 64 from Jul 2026
- 50% tax concession on penalty-free withdrawals
- 10-year window from first penalty-free withdrawal
- Tax-free strategy: ~$40K/year with no other income = $0 tax
- Early withdrawal: 100% taxable + 5% penalty
- Remaining balance after 10 years: 50% taxable lump sum

## AGE 65: CPF LIFE & Government Support

### 1. CPF LIFE (Mandatory if RA >= $60K)

**Three plans:**

| Feature | Standard | Escalating | Basic |
|---------|----------|------------|-------|
| Payout | Flat/steady | Grows 2%/yr | Lower, decreases when RA < $60K |
| Initial amount | Highest | Lower initially | Lower |
| Inflation hedge | No | Yes | No |
| Bequest | Lower | Lower | Higher |
| Default | Yes | No | No |

**Deferral bonus (up to age 70):** ~7% increase per year
- Age 66: +7%, Age 67: +14%, Age 68: +21%, Age 69: +28%, Age 70: +35%

**20% lump sum withdrawal at 65:** Up to 20% of RA (inclusive of $5K already taken at 55)

### 2. Silver Support Scheme (Automatic, quarterly)
- Eligibility: 65+, SC, household monthly income per person <= $2,300, total CPF contributions by 55 <= $140K
- Quarterly payouts:

| HDB Type | Income <= $1,500 | Income $1,500-$2,300 |
|----------|------------------|---------------------|
| 1-2 Room | $1,080 | $540 |
| 3-Room | $860 | $430 |
| 4-Room | $650 | $325 |
| 5-Room | $430 | $215 |

- Paid in CASH (not CPF)

### 3. Lease Buyback Scheme (LBS)
- Eligibility: 65+, SC, HDB, household income <= $14K, no second property, >= 20 years remaining lease
- Sell tail-end of lease to HDB, retain 15-35 years
- Proceeds: RA top-up first (single owner -> FRS, joint -> BRS each), then cash up to $100K

**LBS Bonus:**

| Flat Type | Full (RA top-up >= $60K) | Partial |
|-----------|--------------------------|---------|
| 3-room or smaller | $30,000 | $1:$2 |
| 4-room | $15,000 | $1:$2 |
| 5-room or larger | $7,500 | $1:$2 |

### 4. Matched MediSave Scheme (MMSS) - NEW 2026
- Eligibility: 55-70, SC, MA < half BHS (< $39,500 in 2026)
- Dollar-for-dollar on cash top-ups to MA
- Annual cap: $1,000/year
- Pilot 2026-2030
- ~185,000 eligible in 2026

### 5. BHS Freezes at 65
- BHS permanently fixed at prevailing amount when member turns 65

### 6. Healthcare Deductions (Ongoing)
- MediShield Life: Premiums from MA, increase with age
- CareShield Life: Premiums from MA, 4% annual increase (2026-2030)
- Pioneer/Merdeka generation: Additional premium subsidies

### 7. GST Voucher (MediSave)
- Eligible seniors 65+: $150-$450 annually to MA

## AGE 70: Final Milestones

- CPF LIFE auto-starts if not already (Standard Plan default)
- Maximum deferral benefit achieved (35%)
- Contribution rates drop to 12.5% (above 70)
- SRS: 10-year window may end (if started at 62)

---

## CPF Nomination and Bequest (Any age)
- CPF bypasses will entirely
- With valid nomination: Direct to nominees, ~10 working days
- Without: Public Trustee's Office, up to 6 months, admin fees
- CPF LIFE on death: Unused premium balance returned to nominees
- Marriage revokes nomination

## Key Money Flow Summary

```
INFLOWS (50-55):
  Salary -> OA (15%) + SA (11.5%) + MA (10.5%)
  Cash -> SA top-up (RSTU relief)
  Government -> WIS

AT 55 TRANSITION:
  SA -> RA (up to FRS)
  Excess SA -> OA
  OA -> RA (if SA didn't fill FRS)

INFLOWS (55-70):
  Salary -> OA + RA + MA (declining rates)
  Cash -> RA top-up (RSTU relief)
  OA -> RA transfer (voluntary)
  Government -> MRSS match
  VHR -> RA (then OA)
  LBS proceeds -> RA (then cash)
  MA overflow -> RA -> OA

OUTFLOWS (55+):
  OA withdrawal (above FRS)
  MediShield Life premiums <- MA
  CareShield Life premiums <- MA

OUTFLOWS (65+):
  CPF LIFE payouts <- RA
  Silver Support <- Government (cash)
  20% RA lump sum withdrawal

SRS (62+):
  SRS -> Cash (50% taxable)
```
