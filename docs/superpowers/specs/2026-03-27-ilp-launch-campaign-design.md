# ILP Calculator Launch Campaign: "Check Before You Sign"

**Date:** 2026-03-27
**Status:** Design approved
**Budget:** SGD $2,350-2,550
**Timeline:** 4-6 weeks (target launch: May-June 2026)
**Product URL:** sgfireplanner.com/ilp

## 1. Campaign Overview

Launch campaign for the ILP fee calculator targeting fresh Singapore university graduates in the window between final exams (April-May) and starting work (Aug-Sep), when insurance agents actively approach them. The campaign combines outrage-driven product discovery ("The ILP Receipt") with a meme-based attention layer ("Don't Sign Until You Check") and a Gleam.io giveaway to incentivize sharing.

### What this campaign is NOT

- Not a comprehensive fireplanner marketing push (broader campaign comes later)
- Not anti-insurance (insurance is fine; ILPs specifically have a fee transparency problem)
- Not preachy or lecture-y (peer discovery, not top-down education)

### V2 backlog

- ILP BS Detector quiz: a 60-second quiz scoring ILP literacy, shareable results ("I scored 9/10"). Research and build alongside this campaign, launch as a follow-up.

## 2. Target Audience

Fresh Singapore university graduates (NUS, NTU, SMU, SIT, SUSS, SUTD) who have completed or are completing final exams and have not yet started full-time employment. Age 21-26. The "about to get approached by an insurance agent" moment is the emotional hook.

## 3. Campaign Identity & Positioning

**Campaign name:** "Check Before You Sign"

**Positioning:** Fireplanner is the friend who actually knows finance, not the financial advisor trying to hit MDRT. The brand arms fresh grads with a tool their insurance agent hopes they never find.

**Tone ladder:**

1. **Hook** (first 2 seconds / headline): Edgy, confrontational. "Your insurance agent doesn't want you to see this."
2. **Body** (the explanation): Peer-to-peer casual. "So basically, I ran this ILP through a fee calculator and..."
3. **Punchline/CTA**: SG meme energy. "Don't be the friend who signed without checking."

**Core emotional triggers:**

- **Outrage:** "They take HOW much in fees?"
- **Social protection:** "I need to warn my friends about this"
- **FOMO:** "Everyone's checking their ILP before signing, are you still going in blind?"

## 4. The ILP Receipt -- Product Mechanic

After a user looks up an ILP product on the calculator, a "Generate Receipt" button creates a downloadable/shareable image. The receipt format exposes the fee structure over 25 years in a familiar receipt layout.

### Receipt format (example for a $300/month ILP over 25 years)

```
+-------------------------------------+
|         YOUR ILP RECEIPT            |
|         -----------------           |
| Product: [Insurer] [Plan Name]      |
|                                     |
| You pay:              $90,000       |
|                                     |
| What you keep:        $61,200       |
| What they keep:       $28,800       |
|                       -------       |
| Fee drag:               32%         |
|                                     |
| Same $ in index fund: $104,500      |
| (global equity, 7% gross return)    |
| You're leaving on     -------       |
| the table:            $43,300       |
|                                     |
| ----------------------------------- |
| Fees as of [Month Year]             |
| Based on published fund charges.    |
| Actual returns vary.                |
| Generated at sgfireplanner.com/ilp  |
| Check before you sign.              |
+-------------------------------------+
```

### Key design decisions

- "What they keep" is the gut-punch line. Not "fees" or "charges."
- Index fund comparison gives concrete opportunity cost, not just abstract percentages.
- No personal data exposed. The receipt shows the product's fees, not the user's financial situation. This solves the shareability problem: you're exposing the insurer, not yourself.
- Branded footer with URL drives traffic back to the calculator.
- Shareable as image: works on IG stories, TikTok, WhatsApp, Telegram without needing to click a link.
- "Fees as of [Month Year]" stamp for data freshness.
- Disclaimer: "Based on published fund charges. Actual returns vary."

### Build scope

A feature on the ILP calculator itself: a "Generate Receipt" button that creates a downloadable/shareable image (canvas or html-to-image). Not a separate app.

## 5. Meme Campaign -- Content Strategy

### The universal SG fresh grad experience

Almost every Singaporean grad gets "the approach": an old NS buddy, JC friend, or random LinkedIn connection invites them for coffee, and it turns into an insurance sales pitch. This is the meme goldmine.

### Content pillars (5-7 posts over 2 weeks)

**Pillar 1: "The Approach" memes (3-4 posts)**

Relatable scenarios every grad recognizes. All memes stay generic (no naming specific insurers):

- "POV: your NS buddy texts 'bro long time no see, let's grab coffee' but his LinkedIn says Financial Consultant"
- "The 3 stages of a financial planning workshop: free food, free notebook, $300/month commitment"
- "Nobody: ... That one friend after passing their CMFAS exam:" [wall of texts]
- Drake meme format: "Catching up with old friends" vs "Catching up with old friends who just joined [insurance company]"

**Pillar 2: "The Reveal" posts (2-3 posts)**

Introduce the calculator by showing actual receipts:

- Carousel post: slide 1 is a meme hook, slides 2-3 show an actual ILP Receipt, final slide is the CTA
- Reel/TikTok: screen recording of someone running an ILP through the calculator, genuine reaction to the fees
- Side-by-side: "What your agent showed you" (projected returns brochure) vs "What the fees actually look like" (the receipt)

**Pillar 3: "Don't Be That Guy" CTA (1 post)**

The giveaway launch post. "Tag 3 friends who need to check before they sign. Win AirPods 4."

### Low priority: AI-generated video

If there is extra capacity, experiment with AI video generation for Pillar 2 reaction-style reels. Not a blocker for launch.

### Posting cadence

- **Week 1:** Meme posts (Pillar 1). Build audience, no product mention yet. Just relatable content.
- **Week 2:** Reveal posts (Pillar 2). Introduce the calculator via receipts.
- **Week 2, day 3-4:** Giveaway launch (Pillar 3). Capitalize on engagement momentum.

### Content creation

Static images and short reels. Made with Canva/CapCut. Memes should feel homemade and authentic, not polished brand content.

### Platform adaptation

- **Instagram:** Carousels for Pillar 2, static posts for Pillar 1, Stories for giveaway entries
- **TikTok:** Reaction-style reels for Pillar 2, trending audio + text overlay for Pillar 1
- **Reddit:** Text posts with genuine educational framing. See Section 7 for Reddit-specific strategy.
- **Telegram:** Drop memes + calculator link in uni/grad groups with casual "thought this was useful" framing

## 7. Ignition Strategy -- The First 48 Hours

Starting audience: ~160 email subscribers (expense tracker list), ~70 Telegram subscribers. Not enough to self-ignite. External ignition required.

### Hour 0: Simultaneous multi-channel drop

**Reddit (educational, independent of giveaway):**

- Post on r/singaporefi: genuine discovery-style post. Lead with the insight, not the tool. E.g., "I compared the fee structures of 10 popular ILPs and the results were eye-opening." Share actual data/findings as text in the post body. Calculator link mentioned once at the bottom: "I put the data into a tool if anyone wants to check other products."
- Post on r/SGExams, r/NUS: frame around the fresh grad moment. "For those about to start work and getting approached by insurance agents, here's a tool to check what they're actually selling you."
- Message sub mods beforehand for approval if the sub requires it.
- Zero mention of giveaway, Instagram, or campaign. Reddit is a completely separate channel.
- Fallback: if standalone posts are rejected, contribute as helpful comments on existing ILP threads.

**Reddit hard rules:**

- No astroturfing with multiple accounts
- No marketing language ("check out", "we launched", "we built")
- No links to giveaway or social pages
- No naming specific insurers in post titles

**Telegram:**

- Drop in 5-10 uni/grad Telegram groups with casual framing + calculator link + one meme image
- Existing 70 Telegram subscribers get a direct message with calculator link + giveaway announcement

**Email:**

- 160-person list gets a short email: "Before you sign anything your insurance agent puts in front of you, check this first." Link to calculator + giveaway page.

**Paid boost ($200-300):**

- Boost the single best-performing Pillar 1 meme from Week 1
- Target: Singapore, 21-26, university-educated, interests in personal finance / graduating / new job
- Objective: reach, not conversions. The giveaway post does the conversion.

### Hour 24-48: Momentum plays

- Repost organic engagement (Reddit comments, DMs, shares) as IG Stories
- Crosspost Reddit discussion links to Telegram if they gain traction
- Second ad boost if first is performing ($100-200)

## 10. Success Metrics

The existing ILP tools already have 10-15K users. Raw calculator visits is the wrong primary metric. The campaign's value is new audience acquisition and building a re-engageable social/email following for the broader fireplanner campaign.

### Minimum viable (campaign was worth it)

- 300+ Gleam entries
- 200+ new IG/TikTok followers (people who didn't know fireplanner before)
- 100+ ILP Receipt shares on social media
- 50+ referral chain entries (people brought by other people, not by you)

### Strong performance (consider scaling)

- 1,000+ Gleam entries
- 500+ new social followers
- 300+ receipt shares
- 200+ referral chain entries
- Email list grows by 100+

### Viral breakout indicators (double down immediately)

- Receipt format gets shared organically beyond Gleam entries
- Finance influencer or SG meme page reshares content
- "Check Before You Sign" phrase gets used unprompted

### Key efficiency metric

Cost per new engaged follower. Target: $5/follower (competitive for SG fintech). If above $17/follower, the campaign is too expensive relative to targeted ads.

## 11. Legal & Compliance

### Lucky draw regulations (RESOLVED)

Under the Gambling Control Act 2022 class licence regime (effective 1 Feb 2023), this giveaway qualifies automatically. No permit application or GRA notification required.

**Qualifying conditions (all met):**

1. Organiser is a business entity
2. Promotion is for non-gambling goods/services
3. No fees to participate (free entry)
4. No prohibited formats

**Operational requirements:**

- Notify winners within 7 days of draw
- Keep records for 5 years (entry list, draw record, winner notification)
- Total prizes under SGD $10,000: no independent scrutineer or newspaper publication needed

### PDPA compliance

- **Privacy Policy page** on sgfireplanner.com before campaign launches. Covers: who collects data, what it's used for, retention period, cross-border processing (Gleam.io is Australian).
- **Explicit consent** for newsletter opt-in: separate, optional Gleam action. Not bundled with contest entry. Pre-checked boxes are invalid.
- **Retention policy:** 6 months post-draw for prize delivery/disputes, then delete.
- **Unsubscribe:** honor within 10 business days.
- **Data minimisation:** collect only name + email for entry. Social handles only where needed for specific actions.

### MAS financial promotion

The calculator presents published fee data, not financial advice. Receipts include disclaimer: "Based on published fund charges. Actual returns vary." Fireplanner is positioned as an informational tool, not a licensed financial advisor.

### Platform disclaimers

Every giveaway post includes: "This promotion is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Instagram, TikTok, or Meta."

### Terms and conditions (required elements)

- Organiser name and promoted service
- Draw date, time (SGT), and method
- Rules for participation
- Eligibility (Singapore resident, minimum age 18)
- Entry period (start and end dates in SGT)
- Prize description, quantity, approximate value
- Whether prizes are transferable / cash equivalent offered
- Right to disqualify fraudulent entries or bots
- Right to cancel or modify giveaway
- Governing law: Singapore
- PDPA / Privacy Policy link
- Platform non-affiliation disclaimer

## 12. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|------|----------|------------|
| Insurance industry pushback | Medium | Memes stay generic (no brand names). Receipts use published factual data with disclaimer. Don't attack agents personally; attack fee opacity. |
| Reddit mods reject posts | Medium | Message mods beforehand. Fallback: contribute as helpful comments on existing ILP threads. |
| Low giveaway quality (bots, prize-hunters) | Medium | Receipt screenshot action forces real product engagement. Referral tracking catches duplicates. |
| Best meme doesn't land | Low-Medium | Batch 5-7 memes upfront, test 2-3 in Week 1 before boosting. Don't put all budget behind one post. |
| Fee data challenged as inaccurate | Low | Data sourced from official fund factsheets. "Fees as of [Month Year]" stamp. Pre-launch data review. Discontinued products excluded. |
| Meta/TikTok reject financial ads | Medium | Stricter review for finance content. Submit ads early. Have backup creatives ready. Worst case: organic only. |
| Post-giveaway follower drop-off | Medium | Industry average 30-50% unfollow after winners announced. Plan for minimal content cadence (1 post/week) post-campaign. Email capture builds owned list independent of social algorithms. |
| Calculator not ready in time | High | Receipt feature is the only new build. If it slips, launch meme campaign (Weeks 1-2) while receipt feature catches up. Giveaway can reference calculator without the receipt image. |
| No post-campaign content plan | Medium | Minimum 1 post/week for 4 weeks after campaign. Planned in advance, not ad hoc. |
| PDPA non-compliance | High | Privacy policy, explicit consent, retention policy all set up before campaign launches. See Section 11. |
| Naming specific insurers in memes | High | Hard rule: memes generic only. Only the calculator/receipt names specific products where data is factual. |
