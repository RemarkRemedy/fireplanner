Key design decisions

  • Card 1 (Intro): Both names, both ages, "together" language
  • Card 2 (Net Worth): Combined total as hero, per-person breakdown bars below
  • Card 3 (FIRE Number): Single household number, "both of you" copy
  • Card 4 (Progress): One ring for combined progress, joint encouragement
  • Card 5 (Milestones): Only card with side-by-side per-person view. Shows both FIRE ages + a joint summary line
  • Card 6 (Trajectory): Single combined household portfolio line
  • Card 7 (Peak): Household peak with both ages noted
  • Card 8 (Summary): "Both free by" replaces individual FIRE age. All stats are household-level
