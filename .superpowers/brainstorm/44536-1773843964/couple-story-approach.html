Couple FIRE Story: How should it feel?

The individual story has 8 cards. For a couple, what's the right structure?

A

Joint Story (5-6 cards)

Everything is "we" and "together". Combined net worth, combined FIRE number, joint progress ring, shared milestone ("You'll both be free at 52"), one trajectory chart with two lines. Shorter, feels unified.

Cards: Intro ("Hey [Name] & [Partner]") → Combined Net Worth → Joint FIRE Number → Progress → Joint Trajectory → Summary
B

Split Reveal (6-7 cards)

Start joint (combined net worth), then split: "Your path" card shows your FIRE age, "Partner's path" shows theirs, then merge back for combined trajectory and summary. Creates a narrative arc: together → individual → together again.

Cards: Intro → Combined NW → Your FIRE Age → Partner's FIRE Age → Joint Progress → Trajectory (2 lines) → Summary
C

Comparison Cards (7-8 cards)

Each metric card shows both people side-by-side. Net worth: you vs partner breakdown. FIRE age: your 48 vs their 52. Progress: two rings. More data-dense but makes differences visible. Same length as individual but richer.

Cards: Intro → NW (side-by-side) → FIRE Numbers (both) → Progress (two rings) → Milestones (both ages) → Trajectory (2 lines) → Peak → Summary
