## Fee Structure

### StashAway

StashAway uses a tiered fee structure based on total invested amount:

- **0.8%** for the first S$25,000
- **0.7%** for the next S$25,000
- **0.6%** for the next S$50,000
- Fees continue to decrease as your portfolio grows
- **0.2%** for amounts above S$1,000,000

On top of the management fee, underlying ETF expense ratios typically range from 0.1% to 0.3%.

### Syfe

Syfe charges a flat management fee depending on the portfolio:

- **Core portfolios**: 0.35% - 0.65% (tiered by AUM)
- **Syfe REIT+**: 0.65%
- **Syfe Select (thematic)**: 0.65%

Syfe's pricing tends to be more competitive for smaller portfolios under S$50,000.

## Portfolio Options

StashAway's core offering is its **ERAA (Economic Regime-based Asset Allocation)** framework, which automatically adjusts your portfolio based on macroeconomic indicators. You choose a risk level from 6.5% to 36%, and StashAway handles the asset allocation.

Syfe offers more variety:

- **Core Equity100 / Balanced / Defensive**: Traditional risk-based portfolios
- **Syfe REIT+**: Focused on Singapore REITs with a dividend-oriented approach
- **Syfe Select**: Thematic portfolios (e.g., China Growth, Global ARI)

If you want a set-and-forget approach, StashAway's ERAA is straightforward. If you want more control over themes or want dedicated REIT exposure, Syfe offers more flexibility.

## SRS Integration

Both platforms accept SRS contributions, which can provide meaningful tax savings. If your annual income is above S$80,000, contributing to SRS and investing through either platform could save you several thousand dollars in taxes annually.

## Which One Is Right for You?

**Consider StashAway if:**
- You prefer a single, fully automated investment approach
- Your portfolio is above S$100,000 (lower effective fees)
- You value ERAA's macroeconomic-based rebalancing

**Consider Syfe if:**
- You want access to thematic or REIT-focused portfolios
- Your portfolio is under S$50,000 (potentially lower fees)
- You want more portfolio customisation options

Both platforms are MAS-licensed and offer solid options for passive investing in Singapore. The best choice depends on your investment size, preferences, and whether you want thematic exposure.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use both StashAway and Syfe?

Yes. Many investors split their portfolios across both platforms to diversify their robo advisor risk and take advantage of each platform's strengths.

### Are robo advisors better than DIY investing?

Robo advisors charge management fees that DIY investors avoid. However, they provide automatic rebalancing, disciplined portfolio management, and remove the behavioural risk of panic selling. For most investors, the convenience justifies the cost.

### How do robo advisor fees compare to unit trusts?

Robo advisor fees (0.2% - 0.8%) are significantly lower than most unit trust sales charges (1% - 5%) and annual management fees (1% - 2%).
